The purpose

A useful first step for mission-driven leaders.

Oaktree Institute’s mission is to develop value-driven, action-focused, results-oriented leaders by providing solutions and consulting services for Muslim nonprofits.

The Oaktree Sales Assistant helps nonprofit leaders and staff explore whether they have questions about organizational consulting. It listens for the organization’s needs, answers service questions only when supported by information available on Oaktree’s website, and helps prepare an inquiry for a person to follow up.

What to expect

Clear, careful, and focused on your inquiry.

The assistant stays within its role and is upfront when it cannot confirm something from the information available to it.

01

Share what’s going on

Describe your nonprofit’s challenge and goals in your own words. The assistant asks concise follow-up questions about the support you are looking for.

02

Get grounded answers

Service information comes from available Oaktree website content. If something is unknown or unsupported, the assistant says so instead of guessing.

03

Prepare for a person

The goal is a relevant, understandable inquiry for human follow-up—not an automated consulting recommendation or a final decision about fit.
